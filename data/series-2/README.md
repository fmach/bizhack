**Data Serie**  
This dataset contains a time-series record of water flow and consumption in a school building over 5 calendar days. The data captures the standard operational profile as well as an anomaly detected on the first day.

**Format**:
* timestamp,Datetime,"DD.MM.YYYY hh:mm:ss (e.g., 01.01.2000 00:00:25)"
* flow,Integer,l/min

**Data Characteristics**:
* **Timeframe**: 5 days
* **Day 1 (Anomaly)**: Water leak detected (sustained flow rate outside operational hours).
* **Days 2–5**: Standard operational profile (peak usage during school hours, near-zero night consumption).
