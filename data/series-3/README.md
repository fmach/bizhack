**Data Serie**
This dataset contains a time-series record of water flow in a school building. The data captures standard operational behavior along with an extended water leak occurring during the second and third days (persisting through the night into the fourth day).

**Format**
* timestamp,Datetime,"DD.MM.YYYY hh:mm:ss (e.g., 01.01.2000 00:00:25)"
* flow,Integer,Main inlet flow rate (flowmeter pulse factor: k = 1 l/pulse)

**Data Characteristics**
* **Building Type:** Educational facility (school)
* **Anomaly:** Water leak starting on Day 2 and continuing through Day 3 into the night before Day 4
* **Regular Operation:** Standard consumption patterns outside of the leak period (daytime usage peaks, minimal night consumption)
