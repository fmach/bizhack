**Data Serie**
This dataset contains a time-series record of water flow in a public building. The data captures high-density daily usage patterns along with unexpected overnight water flow events on the second and fifth days.

**Format**
* timestamp,Datetime,"DD.MM.YYYY hh:mm:ss (e.g., 01.01.2000 00:00:25)"
* flow,Integer,Main inlet flow rate (flowmeter pulse factor: k = 100 l/pulse)

**Data Characteristics**
* **Building Type:** Public facility
* **Occupancy:** Approximately 500–1,000 visitors/people daily
* **Overnight Flow Events:** Unexpected continuous flow detected overnight on Day 2 and Day 5
* **Regular Operation:** High-volume operational profiles during regular daytime hours
